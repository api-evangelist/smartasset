You may inherit a Roth IRA and think you can leave the money invested indefinitely because qualified withdrawals are generally tax-free. Tax-free withdrawals do not eliminate the IRS rules for inherited accounts. In most cases, non-spouse beneficiaries must empty an inherited Roth IRA within a set period of time.
